# Every bank is org, project and area; a team bank is created on any forge, reviewed by the owners its manifest names, and joined from a preview

Decided 2026-09-28 on the map ticket "Team bank flow: owner picker, entities, duplicate switch, teammate join" (david/agent-harness issue 46), on the templates of ADR 0034 and the personal flow of ADR 0035. The worked team bank, meadowstudios, is a private GitHub repository under David's own user with one collaborator, kept on GitHub rather than the primary forge because that is where the team can reach it; its folders are `brands/{brand}/{system}/`, while notebook's are `projects/{org}/{project}/`, and a free private repository offers no branch protection or code owners, so the forge cannot hold a reviewed change back. Three decisions follow, one about every bank and two about team banks.

**One folder structure for every bank**, replacing ADR 0034's two levels: `projects/{org}/{project}/{area}/`. The org level is always present (a personal bank's `personal` and each company; a team's single org named for the team, or one per department); the area level is optional. Memories live at a project or in one of its areas, with topics one level down inside either, so a product line stays a topic. An org folder has an `ORG.md` with `line:`, a project its `PROJECT.md`, an area an `AREA.md` (which replaces `SYSTEM.md`), each carrying `line:`, and a project or area `topics:` and `repos:`. The rendered trail has one breadcrumb per project or area that holds memories, grouped under a header per org, and the org header is the pointer that may replace its group when the tier's cap is reached (ADR 0013). Memory tools scope a draft by `{org, project}` or `{org, project, area}`. notebook fits as it is and only gains `ORG.md` files; meadowstudios moves to `projects/meadowstudios/<brand>/<system>/`, with the holding company's shared facts under `projects/meadowstudios/holding/<system>/`, in the memory-banks build's migration.

**Creating a team bank** goes through a forge picker, any forge with a verified account, preset to the primary, then an owner picker, the user or an organisation they can create repositories in, read live through the ForgeService (ADR 0020). The repository is always private, its name is proposed from the team name and editable, and the card warns that every teammate needs an account on that forge; there is no local-only team bank. The wizard asks the team's name, which becomes the first org (departments are added as more orgs), and its first projects, each of which becomes an entity with a `folder:` and a `PROJECT.md`, written in the first commit as ADR 0035's create does. The describe conversation (ADR 0019) then adds aliases, the first areas and up to five orientation memories in the bank's home folder, `projects/<first org>/bank/`. **`owners:` in `BANK.md`** lists forge logins, preset to the creator, and a change to the list is itself a reviewed change. Memories merge on their own; a change ADR 0035's merge rule holds for review (orientation, decisions, a status, the manifest) is merged by the BankService only once an owner other than its author has approved it on the forge, and while the bank has a single owner, that owner merges such changes themselves.

**Bringing teammates in** is two links on the creator's card: "Invite teammates on <forge>", opening the repository's collaborator or organisation-member page, and a join link to send. The forge stays the only membership record. A teammate who pastes the link sees a preview before anything is attached: the bank line, the orgs, projects and entities, the orientation names, the owners, the merge rule, the shared-bank rules (no personal facts, no secrets), and whether their forge account can read or push. They tick the accounts to attach, none preset; the entry is read-write when they can push and read-only otherwise, never their default write target, with the private-copy switch off. A private copy in a personal bank stays a pointer to the team memory (ADR 0034), so the team's copy is the one kept current and a person who leaves the team loses the facts with the access.

## Considered options

- The primary forge only, as the map's re-scope read, or organisations only: rejected; meadowstudios lives off the primary because its team is there, and a user-owned private repository with collaborators is a working team bank.
- Two levels named per team, two fixed templates, a project-and-area path with the org as a field, a free tree of folders, or levels declared per bank: rejected for one path with an always-present org, which keeps notebook unchanged, gives departments real folders and needs no per-bank shape.
- The forge's code owners with branch protection, or the creator as the only reviewer: rejected; unavailable on a free private repository and different on each forge, or broken once the creator is the author or has left.
- Joining with no preview, or with every account preset: not chosen; David wants the teammate to see the bank's contract and pick the accounts.
- A private copy that restates the team fact: rejected; the copies drift and company facts leave with the person.

## Consequences

- The memory-banks specification (issue 90) carries the structure (the three levels, `ORG.md` and `AREA.md`, the scope labels, the org-grouped trail), the team create flow with its forge and owner pickers, `owners:` and the review rule, the join preview and its registry defaults, and the migration of meadowstudios's folders and of `SYSTEM.md` to `AREA.md`; the Set up specification (issue 88) holds the team card, its two links and the preview. The personal flow of ADR 0035 is unchanged but for the optional area level.
- Health of a team bank is a personal bank's, plus needs attention when an owner's login no longer resolves on the forge.
- The team bank's `BANK.md` gains `owners:`; the template's body and paths are rewritten to the new structure, and "brand" remains a word the body may use for a project.
