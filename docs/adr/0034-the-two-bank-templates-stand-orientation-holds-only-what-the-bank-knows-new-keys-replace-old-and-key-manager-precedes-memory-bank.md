# The two bank templates stand: orientation holds only what the bank knows, the new keys replace the old, a product line is a topic, and the Key manager step precedes Memory bank

Decided 2026-09-25 on the map ticket "Prototype: BANK.md templates for a personal and a team bank" (david/agent-harness issue 35), on David's reaction to the drafts on branch `prototype/bank-templates` (`docs/design/bank-templates/`, commit b8c0144, never merged), which carry the personal and team templates with wizard placeholders, Brandsolidate as the worked team example, and a rendered sample of each (bank line, orientation, breadcrumbs, the instruction-layer lines with the entity and duplicate-switch lines, a folder index). Four things the drafting surfaced are decided here. **A bank's orientation memories hold only what the bank itself knows**: its accounts or brands, its secrets layout, where its work is tracked; each is a short pointer within ADR 0013's caps, living in a home folder of the bank's own (`personal/memory-bank/` in a personal bank, `brandsolidate/bank/` in the example), and the key vault and the primary forge are left to the orientation block that ADR 0011 renders from live state, so no fact has two sources; ADR 0010's examples are read that way. **The new keys replace the old**: `BANK.md` has `purpose` (the bank line, at most 160 characters) and no `description`; `PROJECT.md` and `SYSTEM.md` have `line:` (at most 100 characters), `topics:` and `repos:` (remote identities) and no `summary:` or `code:`; an entity carries a `folder:` naming what its match expands; the migration in the memory-banks build renames the old keys in cortex and brandsolidate. **The scope stays two levels and a product line is a topic**: a brand's `product/` is a system whose memories nest one level into topics named for each product line, the migration moving today's `product/<line>` memories into topics. **The Key manager step moves before Memory bank** (David), so a bank is created with the environment's vault known and its secrets rule can name a path from the start: the milestone-1 order is now Account, Carry over, Your machines, Forges, Key manager, Memory bank, Skills, Instructions, Browser, Permissions, Appearance, amending ADR 0016 and ADR 0020; the Move stored tokens card of ADR 0028 keeps covering banks joined later with a stored token.

## Considered options

- Bank orientation repeating the vault and forge facts, as ADR 0010's examples read: rejected; two sources for one fact.
- Keeping the old keys as fallbacks, or new keys without `folder:` on entities: rejected; two ways to say one thing, or a brand whose folder differs from its name left inexpressible.
- The template naming the key-manager path as a placeholder, or the Key manager step patching a bank's `BANK.md` afterwards: not chosen; David moved the step instead, so the vault is known when the bank is written.
- A third scope level, or product lines as systems: rejected; every tier and pointer would grow a case, or the product family would lose its grouping.

## Consequences

- The memory-banks spec (issue 90) carries the two templates as the shipped ones, the key renames and the migration, the orientation home folder, `folder:` on entities and the product-line topics; the Set up spec (issue 88) carries the new step order; the personal and team bank flows (issues 45 and 46) take the draft's remaining open questions (listed on issue 35) as their inputs.
- Chosen defaults for review: breadcrumbs print the full `bank:path/` pointer and memory lines print bare names, the bank and folder named once in the section header (ADR 0013); the `BANK.md` body rides into a run only through the instruction layer's rendered lines, never verbatim; the duplicate-to-personal switch line reads "You may also keep a private copy of a team fact in <personal bank>: off" and a private copy points at the team memory rather than restating it; short aliases like `CJ` are allowed and matched whole-word.
- Fog: who writes a team bank's first orientation memories and reviews its orientation pull requests (the bank flows decide); a local-only bank's landing and follow-ups with no forge.
- Amended by ADR 0037: the scope is three levels, `projects/{org}/{project}/{area}/`, with the area optional; `AREA.md` replaces `SYSTEM.md`; a product line is still a topic.
